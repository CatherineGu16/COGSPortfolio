ABOUT ME



Hey, I'm Catherine!


Welcome to my portfolio! I am a designer based in San Diego, California and am currently pursuing a master's in Human Computer Interaction at UCI.

My design journey started a few quarters into my first year as an undergraduate at UCSD and I have learned so much through my courses and projects. Through the experiences I have gained, I have found the most interest in UX/UI design work and user research. I hope to be able to reach more people with my designs and utilize my skills to develop innovative solutions.
