About

Hey, I'm Catherine!

Welcome to my portfolio! I'm a designer based in San Diego, California, with a master's in Human-Computer Interaction & Design from UCI and a background in Cognitive Science from UCSD.

My design journey started a few quarters into my first year as an undergraduate at UCSD, and I've learned so much through my courses and projects. Through those experiences I've found the most interest in UX/UI design and user research. I hope to reach more people with my designs and use my skills to develop innovative solutions.
